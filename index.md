# Lunar Line Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Lunar Line ("the game") is a free iPhone game by Ercüment Lacin. This page says what data the game and its ad provider handle.

## What the game stores

Your progress (money, machines, Stars, settings, whether you bought Remove Ads) is saved on your device only. The game has no accounts and does not send your progress to any server of ours. We do not collect your name, email address or location.

## Ads

The game shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data.

- On iOS the game asks your permission (App Tracking Transparency) before the advertising identifier is used for tracking. If you refuse, ads are still shown but are not personalised.
- In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and similar regions, the game shows Google's consent form before ads are requested. You can answer it there.

Google's own policy for this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

You can turn tracking off at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

## Purchases

"Remove Ads" is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple (StoreKit). Apple processes the payment; we do not see or store your payment details. The game only learns whether the purchase is owned, to switch ads off.

## Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, is not in the Kids Category, and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
